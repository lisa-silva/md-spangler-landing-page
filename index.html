★ MD Spangler Concrete — Northern California’s Most Experienced Concrete & Civil Engineering Legacy

MD Spangler Concrete isn’t just a contractor — it’s the continuation of a three‑generation concrete dynasty. The Spangler name has been synonymous with structural integrity, engineering‑grade craftsmanship, and uncompromising quality for nearly half a century.

Mel Spangler began working in concrete at just five years old under his father, learning the trade the way true craftsmen do — by doing it, every day, for decades.

A standard of precision. A standard of honesty. A standard of “do it right the first time.”

Over the next 40+ years, Mel became one of Northern California’s most respected concrete specialists — known for structural accuracy, flawless pours, and intuitive problem‑solving earned through a lifetime in the field.
