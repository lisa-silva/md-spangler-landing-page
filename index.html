About MD Spangler Concrete

One Contractor. One Continuous Professional Lineage.

MD Spangler Concrete is a licensed Northern California concrete contractor led by Melvin Dan Spangler, who has operated under CSLB License #598557 since 1990 with dual A (General Engineering) and C-8 (Concrete) classifications.

The company’s history includes its earlier corporate identity, Spangler Concrete & Engineering, Inc., which Mel ran while serving clients across Sunnyvale, Morgan Hill, Santa Rosa, and the greater Bay Area.

Today, MD Spangler Concrete continues that same professional lineage in Redding, delivering high-quality residential and commercial concrete work backed by more than 40 years of hands-on experience. With a long-standing reputation for reliability, engineering-grade standards, and precise workmanship, MD Spangler Concrete provides trusted concrete solutions throughout Shasta, Tehama, and Butte Counties.

One continuous identity: “MD SPANGLER” is the CSLB license name, “MD Spangler Concrete” is the current marketing name, and “Spangler Concrete & Engineering, Inc.” is the earlier corporate identity—all part of Melvin Dan Spangler’s continuous owner-operated business history.
