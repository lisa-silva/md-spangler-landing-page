Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

MD Spangler Concrete respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit this website or contact us about our concrete services.

This website does not include an online contact form, user accounts, or payment processing. We may receive information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide: If you call, text, or email us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, project location, project details, and any other information you choose to provide.

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, communicate about potential or active projects, provide services, maintain business records, operate and secure the website, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that support our website or business operations, when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, or when required by law or needed to protect safety, rights, or property.

Third-party services and links

The embedded map is provided by Google. Google controls its own collection and use of information. Review the Google Privacy Policy for details. This website may also link to other third-party websites; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Cookies and tracking

MD Spangler Concrete does not place first-party advertising or analytics cookies through this website. Third-party content, including Google Maps, may use cookies or similar technologies subject to the third party's policies and your settings.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including project administration, business records, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your privacy choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided or to request that it be corrected or deleted. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to retain certain information for legal or legitimate business reasons. Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have additional rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or information about data practices.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.
